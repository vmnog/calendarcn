Every pull request merged to `main` goes live in the registry right away, so this page lists changes by the day they merged. Find the day you last ran `add`, read up from there, then [update](/docs/installation#updating). Each entry names the items whose files changed. Changes to the docs, the demo and the tests are left out.

Entries marked **Breaking** can stop your code from compiling or change what it does after you update. Each one says what to change.

## Unreleased

Live in the registry, but not in a tagged release yet.

### November 10, 2026

- **Added** `calendar-app`, `calendar-core`, `calendar-list`, `calendar-sidebar`: in Calendar App, the eye beside a calendar hides its events from every view and shows them again on a second click. The .ics export still writes every event. `filterShownEvents()` and `mergeShownEvents()` are new in `calendar-core`, to do the same in a layout of your own. [#76](https://github.com/vmnog/calendarcn/pull/76)
  - **Breaking:** `CalendarList` drops `visibilitySoon` and `CalendarSidebar` drops `calendarVisibilitySoon`, both added earlier the same day in #74. If you installed in between and set either one, remove it.
- **Fixed** `command-menu`: the hints beside "Go to next week" and "Go to previous week" now show <Kbd>K</Kbd> and <Kbd>J</Kbd>, the keys that do it. [#75](https://github.com/vmnog/calendarcn/pull/75)
- **Improved** `calendar-app`, `calendar-header`, `calendar-list`, `calendar-sidebar`, `details-sidebar`: controls that did nothing are disabled with a "Soon" badge: Scheduling, Meet with… and Add calendar account in the sidebar, the view settings a view doesn't read, and the useful shortcuts nothing handles (`DetailsSidebarShortcut` takes `soon`). [#74](https://github.com/vmnog/calendarcn/pull/74)
- **Fixed** `calendar-core`, `time-grid`: an event that ends at midnight, like 11 PM to 12 AM, stays on its own day in the hour grid and month view, and dragging it no longer saves an end before its start. [#70](https://github.com/vmnog/calendarcn/pull/70)
- **Fixed** `ics`: `toIcs()` writes a repeating timed event in its time zone, with a `TZID`, instead of in UTC, so it repeats at the same clock time when the clocks change. `parseIcs()` keeps a repeating event's IANA `TZID` as its `timeZone`. One-off events are still written in UTC. [#73](https://github.com/vmnog/calendarcn/pull/73)
- **Improved** `calendar-app`, `calendar-core`, `calendar-header`, `event-details`: Cut, Copy, Duplicate and Delete in the event menus, and "Declined events" in the view settings, are disabled with a "Soon" badge, since nothing handled them. <KbdGroup><Kbd>⇧</Kbd><Kbd>⌘</Kbd><Kbd>D</Kbd></KbdGroup> goes back to the browser. [#72](https://github.com/vmnog/calendarcn/pull/72)
- **Fixed** `calendar-core`: an event whose `timeZone` the runtime doesn't know, like a typo or a Windows zone name, no longer takes the calendar down. It shows in the viewer's zone. `isKnownTimeZone()` is new in `@/lib/time-zones`. [#71](https://github.com/vmnog/calendarcn/pull/71)
- **Fixed** `calendar-core`: on the days the clocks change, the day and week grids draw events on the right hour, a click on an empty slot creates the event at that row's time, and <Kbd>Alt</Kbd> with an arrow key moves an event by clock time. [#69](https://github.com/vmnog/calendarcn/pull/69)
- **Fixed** `calendar-core`, `event-details`: shortcuts no longer run while typing in a `contenteditable` element or with a `<select>` focused, <Kbd>Alt</Kbd> with an arrow key is left to the browser unless an event has focus, and the <Kbd>Enter</Kbd> that confirms an IME composition no longer commits the event title. [#68](https://github.com/vmnog/calendarcn/pull/68)
- **Fixed** `time-grid`, `month-view`: four gestures that saved the wrong dates. Nudging an all-day bar in day view keeps the days off screen, resizing an all-day event to one or two days keeps it all-day, an overnight event can't be resized to end before it starts, and a month bar dragged by its second day lands on the right days. [#65](https://github.com/vmnog/calendarcn/pull/65)

### November 9, 2026

- **Added** `calendar-app`, a new block: the whole live demo as one component, with both sidebars, the header, every view, the ⌘K menu, theme switching and .ics import and export. See [Calendar App](/docs/components/calendar-app). [#62](https://github.com/vmnog/calendarcn/pull/62)
- **Added** `calendar-sidebar`, `details-sidebar`, `mini-calendar`, `calendar-list`, `theme-toggle` and `sidebar-overlay`, new items: the live demo's sidebars, part by part. In `calendar-core`, `CalendarInfo` gains `isSubscribed`, which the calendar list marks with a feed icon. [#61](https://github.com/vmnog/calendarcn/pull/61)
- **Added** `calendar-header` and `command-menu`, new items: the live demo's header and ⌘K menu. `useCalendarShortcuts()` in `calendar-core` takes optional `onToggleCommandMenu`, `onToggleSidebar`, `onToggleContextPanel`, `onCycleTheme` and `onOpenSettings`. `calendar` now builds its toolbar from the header's parts, so updating it installs `calendar-header`, which ships `view-dropdown.tsx` at the same path. [#60](https://github.com/vmnog/calendarcn/pull/60)

### November 7, 2026

- **Added** `conference-providers` and `sample-data`, two new items: placeholder Google Meet, Zoom and Microsoft Teams to pass to `conferenceProviders`, and the live demo's events, calendars, contacts, rooms and places. See [Sample Data](/docs/sample-data). [#58](https://github.com/vmnog/calendarcn/pull/58)
- **Fixed** `event-details`: in a narrow details panel, the time row shortens the duration instead of cutting off the end time. [#56](https://github.com/vmnog/calendarcn/pull/56)
- **Added** `event-details`, `calendar-core`, `month-view`, `agenda-view`, `ics`: a calendar picker, busy/free, visibility and reminders in the details panel. Calendars take `defaultReminders`, and `resolveEventColor`, `moveEventToCalendar` and `addReminder` are new helpers. `.ics` export now writes "1 week before" reminders correctly. [#51](https://github.com/vmnog/calendarcn/pull/51)
  - **Breaking:** an event without a `color` takes its calendar's color, and falls back to blue only when the calendar has none. Set `color` on an event to keep it blue.
  - **Breaking:** an event moved to another calendar gets that calendar's `email`, or none, instead of keeping the old one.
- **Added** `event-details`, `calendar-core`: participants and rooms, conferencing, AI meeting notes and location in the details panel. `CalendarProvider` takes `contacts`, `rooms`, `conferenceProviders` and `locationSuggestions` to fill their menus. The Conferencing row shows once you pass `conferenceProviders`. Installs the shadcn/ui `avatar`. [#53](https://github.com/vmnog/calendarcn/pull/53)
- **Added** `event-details`: links you can copy and remove, "Paste link…" and "Attach file", and a description edited in place. Only `http:` and `https:` links become clickable. [#52](https://github.com/vmnog/calendarcn/pull/52)
- **Added** `event-details`, `calendar-core`, `time-grid`, `ics`: editable time zone and repeat rows. Changing the zone keeps the wall-clock time, repeat offers presets and a custom rule, and arrows step through a series (`previousInSeries` and `nextInSeries` in `useCalendar()`). `@/lib/recurrence` and `@/lib/time-zones` are new in `calendar-core`. Installs the shadcn/ui `command` and `dialog`. [#50](https://github.com/vmnog/calendarcn/pull/50)
  - **Breaking:** `parseIcs()` reads `RRULE` into `recurrenceRule`. The raw `recurrence` string is now kept only for rules it can't parse.
  - **Breaking:** `EventItemPopoverProps` drops `onPrevWeek` and `onNextWeek`, since the series arrows no longer navigate by period.
- **Added** `event-details`, `calendar-core`: Notion Calendar's time menu in 15-minute steps, which also reads typed times (`7`, `930`) and durations, plus a date picker and an all-day switch. Installs the shadcn/ui `calendar` and `popover`, and `react-day-picker`. [#54](https://github.com/vmnog/calendarcn/pull/54)

### November 6, 2026

- **Added** `event-details`, `calendar-core`: the details panel rebuilt on Notion Calendar's layout, one file per section. `DetailedCalendarEvent` gains optional `kind`, `timeZone`, `recurrenceRule`, `participants`, `rooms`, `conference`, `aiMeetingNotes`, `links` and `attachments`. Without `onEventChange` the panel is read-only, and <Kbd>Esc</Kbd> restores a field without deselecting the event. [#49](https://github.com/vmnog/calendarcn/pull/49)
  - **Breaking:** the event type picked in the panel's header is now saved on the event as `kind`, through `onEventChange`. Save `kind` with your events to keep it.
  - **Breaking:** if your event type already has a `kind`, `participants` or `links` field of another type, it no longer fits `DetailedCalendarEvent`. Rename your field.
  - **Deprecated:** `timezone`, a display label. Set `timeZone` to an IANA zone instead.

### November 5, 2026

- **Added** `calendar-core`, `event-details`, `time-grid`, `month-view`, `agenda-view`: support for shadcn/ui projects set up with Base UI, as well as Radix. The details popover positions itself with `@floating-ui/react-dom`, and on Radix, picking an event type no longer closes it. <Contributor handle="doneumark" pr={43} />
- **Fixed** `calendar-core`: `calendar-provider.tsx` type-checks and lints in projects without `@types/node`, like a stock Vite app. <Contributor handle="doneumark" pr={46} />
- **Fixed** `time-grid`, `month-view`: scrolling an event into view no longer shifts the week columns away from their headers, or the month rows. [#44](https://github.com/vmnog/calendarcn/pull/44)

### November 2, 2026

- **Added** `ics`, a new item: `parseIcs()` and `toIcs()` read and write .ics files from Google Calendar, Apple Calendar and Outlook. [#36](https://github.com/vmnog/calendarcn/pull/36)
- **Added** `agenda-view`, a new item, with `calendar-core` and `calendar`: the next 30 days' events grouped by day, as the `"agenda"` view with the <Kbd>A</Kbd> shortcut. [#35](https://github.com/vmnog/calendarcn/pull/35)
  - **Breaking:** `ViewType` gains `"agenda"`. A `switch` that handles every view needs a case for it.
- **Added** `calendar-core`, `time-grid`, `month-view`: `onVisibleRangeChange` reports the dates on screen as people navigate, and `loading` shows a progress bar while events arrive. [#34](https://github.com/vmnog/calendarcn/pull/34)

### November 1, 2026

- **Added**: the registry's first items, `calendar`, `calendar-core`, `time-grid`, `day-view`, `week-view`, `month-view` and `event-details`, served from `calendarcn.xyz/r` for shadcn/ui projects set up with Radix. [#37](https://github.com/vmnog/calendarcn/pull/37), on the library groundwork from [#31](https://github.com/vmnog/calendarcn/pull/31).
