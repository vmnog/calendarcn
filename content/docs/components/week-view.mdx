Week View shows several days side by side on the [Time Grid](/docs/components/time-grid). It defaults to a full week and can show any number of days from 2 to 9.

## Features

- Day headers that highlight today.
- Scroll sideways to move through days. Scrolling snaps to the nearest day.
- Hide weekends to focus on working days.
- In the demo, press <Kbd>W</Kbd> or <Kbd>0</Kbd> for the week view and <Kbd>2</Kbd> to <Kbd>9</Kbd> to pick the number of days.
