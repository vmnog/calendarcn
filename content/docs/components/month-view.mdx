Month View shows a grid of weeks. Multi-day events run across days as bars, and busy days collapse extra events into a "more" link. In the demo, press <Kbd>M</Kbd> to switch to it.

## Features

- Multi-day events drawn as continuous bars across the week.
- A "more" link on days with more events than fit.
- Drag an event to move it to another day, or drag its ends to change its dates.
