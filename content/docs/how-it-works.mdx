## The provider

`CalendarProvider` holds everything the calendar knows: the events, the view and the date it shows, which events are selected, and the event being created. It also does every change: a drag, a resize, a paste or a typed title all end in the provider, which updates its events and calls your handlers (`onEventsChange`, `onEventChange`, `onEventCreate`). Undo and notices live there too, so every view gets them for free.

`<Calendar>` is the provider with a toolbar and the view the provider names. Use the provider yourself when you want your own layout, your own toolbar or more than one view on screen.

## The views

A view only draws. `DayView`, `WeekView`, `MonthView` and `AgendaView` read the provider's state, draw it, and turn what you do with the pointer or the keys back into calls to the provider. ## Build your own view

Any component inside a `CalendarProvider` can call `useCalendar()` and get the same state and actions the built-in views use. Read `events` and `visibleRange` to draw, and call `updateEvent`, `selectEvent` or `startEventDraft` when someone acts on what you drew. The [`useCalendar()` reference](/docs/reference/api#usecalendar) lists every value and action.
