## Creating events

New events start the way they do in Notion Calendar, as a draft:

- **Double-click** an empty slot in the day or week view for a 30-minute draft there, or **press and drag** to draw one of any length: down, up, or across day columns. Both ends snap to `snapMinutes`.
- Do the same in the all-day row or in the month view for an all-day draft over one day or several.
- On touch, a long press starts the draft, and dragging on draws it.
- A single click on empty space creates nothing. It clears the selection.

A draft is drawn and selected, with its title focused in the details, but it isn't in `events` yet. It is saved by its first edit: a title (on <Kbd>Enter</Kbd>, or when focus leaves the title) or a change to any other field. Saving adds it to `events`, calls `onEventCreate` once and is one step for [undo](/docs/guides/undo-and-notices#undo-and-redo). A draft that nothing was typed into is thrown away when the selection moves on: <Kbd>Esc</Kbd> (once to leave the title, once more for the draft), a click elsewhere, or another event. <Kbd>Esc</Kbd> in the middle of a drag drops it too.

While nothing is typed into it, a draft is a tinted range in its calendar's color. Its first title keystroke turns it into an event block that shows the title as you type.

- `createEvent` builds the draft from its range. Return an untitled event, as the default does, for Notion Calendar's flow, or `null` to create nothing there. The range's `isAllDay` is set for whole days.
- `defaultEventDuration` sets the length of a double-clicked event, in minutes.
- <Kbd>C</Kbd> and the command menu's "Create event..." start a draft at the
  next half hour (`addEventAtNextSlot()`), drawn as an event block from the
  start, as in Notion Calendar.
- `useCalendar()` returns the draft as `eventDraft` and starts one from your own button or command with `startEventDraft(range)`, drawn the same way. `updateEvent(draft)` saves it, `updateEventDraft(draft)` changes it without saving, and `clearSelection()` throws it away.
- `addEventFromRange(range)` still adds an event at once, with no draft.

[A New event button](#a-new-event-button) starts drafts from buttons of your own.
